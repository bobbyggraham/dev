OFFICIAL RULEBOOK — HOW THE GAME IS SCORED
HIT
An open opportunity with every field that matters current: follow-up date set, close date real and in the future, value above $0, touched in the last 90 days.
AT-BAT
Every open opportunity. Each one is a plate appearance — you own it, you swing at it.
BATTING AVG
Hits ÷ at-bats. Minimum 30 at-bats to qualify — below the bar you post a .000 until you carry a real book. No batting titles on two swings.
K — STRIKEOUT
Open opportunity with no estimated close date. These dollars can't land in any quarter until the field is fixed.
LOB
Left on base: close date already in the past, deal still open. Runners stranded.
E — ERROR
Open opportunity with $0 or blank value — invisible in every dollar roll-up. Enter $1 or a humble estimate.
ON BASE
Open dollars with a real, future close date — pipeline that can actually score in a quarter.
CORKED BAT
A Closed Won deal that lived 14 days or less in Engage before closing — logged at the handshake, not run through the pipeline. A win-integrity flag, not a ranking stat.
