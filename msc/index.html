The Process

Six steps from first conversation to a full turnkey scope of work.

1
Qualify

Confirm turnkey intent

Verify the customer is looking for a full turnkey lighting retrofit — not just a supply-only purchase or partial upgrade.

2
Scope Check

Does a scope of work already exist?

If a competitive scope exists, FSG jumps straight to the proposal phase and delivers a cost proposal to MSC with recommended next steps — apples to apples. No scope yet? Move to Step 3.

3
Information Gathering

Collect the minimum required info

Facility address, approximate square footage, a copy of an electric bill, and a general description of the space and what lighting is currently installed. That's enough for FSG to produce an estimate.

4
Estimate Delivery

FSG submits estimate + audit fee

An estimate of MSC cost is submitted, along with a cost to send a lighting estimator to site to draft a formal scope of work.

Note: Audit fees are waived if the project moves forward within 90 days or is otherwise imminent.
5
Approval

Customer approves estimate

Once the customer approves, the on-site lighting audit is scheduled and completed by FSG.

6
Deliverable

FSG submits full turnkey scope of work

FSG delivers a complete turnkey scope of work to MSC, ready for customer presentation and project execution.

What to Gather On Site

The form walks you through all of it — required first, bonus after.

Required

  • Facility address
  • Approximate square footage of the area to be retrofitted
  • Copy of an electric bill
  • General description of the space and what lighting is currently installed

Bonus — Improves Estimate Accuracy

  • Current fixture types (2x4, strips, downlights, etc.) — photos are extremely helpful
  • Voltage of the space
  • Any fixtures not working?
  • Existing control systems (switch, timer, occupancy sensor, etc.)
  • Typical hours of operation
  • Any light level concerns?
